# Course-Wide Resources

This index collects resources linked from the [Fields Institute course page](https://www.fields.utoronto.ca/activities/26-27/SGC-safety), Professor [Yevgeny Liokumovich's AI safety page](https://www.math.toronto.edu/ylio/), and a small set of papers matched to the weekly syllabus. Weekly folders contain shorter, topic-specific lists.

## Suggested starting points

- [ILIAD Intensive Curriculum](https://www.iliad.ac/intensive) — the course's suggested curriculum for foundational AI alignment research.
- [Introduction to AI Safety, Ethics, and Society](https://www.aisafetybook.com/) by Dan Hendrycks — the course's suggested introductory text.
- [AI Safety for Mathematicians](https://mathforaisafety.org/) — mathematical introduction to the field, maintained by Jacob Tsimerman.
- [Fields course lecture recordings](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZhkBbRD_dJM) — recordings for this course.
- [AI Safety for Mathematicians video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Zn1H0bwB9y4) — linked from the instructor's page.

## Related course notes

- [Mathematics for AI Safety](https://lionellevine.github.io/MAIS.html), Lionel Levine, Cornell University — lecture notes and references from a related mathematical course.
- [AI Safety (CS 2881), Fall 2026](https://boazbk.github.io/mltheoryseminar/), Boaz Barak, Harvard University — lectures on capabilities, training, interpretability, and alignment.
- [AI Alignment](https://alignment-w2024.notion.site/), Roger Grosse, University of Toronto — linked from the Fields page; the page may require Notion access.

## Instructor's AI safety papers

- [Sky sphere representation in language models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27092), Yevgeny Liokumovich and A. Berdnikov (2026 preprint) — geometric representations in language models; relevant to Weeks 05–06.
- [Quantifying stability of non-power-seeking in artificial agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.03529), Evan Ryan Gunter, Yevgeny Liokumovich, and Victoria Krakovna (2024 preprint) — studies when non-shutdown-avoiding behavior remains stable under changes in an environment; relevant to Week 10.

## Research and community organizations

These are research, training, and community entry points listed on the instructor's page; their programs and availability can change.

- [Mathematical AI Safety Institute (MAISI)](https://maisi.org/) — mathematical AI safety research.
- [Fields Institute Centre for Mathematical AI](https://www.fields.utoronto.ca/centres/centre-mathematical-ai) — research themes, seminars, courses, and the joint Mathematics for AI Safety fellowship program.
- [Toronto AI Safety Initiative (TAISI)](https://www.taisi.ca/) — Toronto research training and student community.
- [Trajectory Labs](https://www.trajectorylabs.org/) — Toronto AI safety workspace and research community.
- [Fields–PrincInt postdoctoral fellowships in Mathematics for AI Safety](https://www.mathjobs.org/jobs/FIELDS/PDFAI) — example research directions include interpretability, formal verification, and AI R&D automation risks.
